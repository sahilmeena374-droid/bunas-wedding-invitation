# Bunara Family Wedding Invitation

Animation mode: animated-website

## Audience and intent
A private, share-by-link digital companion to the already-printed family wedding invitation. Guests should immediately understand that the family is celebrating three weddings across two dates. The website is a two-part digital invitation that echoes the physical card rather than replacing it.

## Design direction
Traditional Rajasthani wedding stationery: warm ivory paper, deep maroon ink, antique muted gold, delicate floral corner ornaments, Ganesha medallion, subtle paper grain and restrained gold foil lighting. Display type: Cormorant Garamond for the heritage/editorial wedding mood; body: DM Sans for legibility. Keep the page light, warm, ceremonial and uncluttered. Use one maroon accent consistently. No dark tech aesthetic, no gradients-as-decoration, no generic stock wedding photos.

## Journey
Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous 15-second film, scrubbed as the visitor scrolls; the single hero subject is the printed invitation world and its Ganesha medallion. A single subject and continuous camera move suit the invitation better than multiple unrelated scenes.

Chapters:
1. The welcome: full invitation cover, headline “Shubh Vivaah”, establish the family invitation and the two wedding dates.
2. The cover: present the printed front cover and the three pairings: Nisha–Rohit (Khyali), Vandana–Kartik, Kishor (Karuna)–Anuradha.
3. The inside invitation: two inside panels, matching the physical card. The first panel contains the two 24 November weddings; the second panel contains Kishor–Anuradha on 26 November.
4. The celebrations: readable event programme timelines for 24 November and 26 November, transcribed from the printed card.
5. The venue: Kotdi Dhayalan, Sikar, Rajasthan, with the map link encoded in the printed QR code.

World grammar: ivory handmade paper, deep maroon lettering, antique muted gold, restrained floral linework, warm light from upper-left, realistic paper/foil texture, centered focal subject and ample negative space. One continuous, slow camera push/glide, locked exposure, no cuts, no on-screen generated text.

Mobile framing: keep the Ganesha motif and invitation focal area centered; use a lighter mobile encode and mobile-specific poster.

## Privacy and delivery
No community-feed listing. Add noindex/nofollow metadata so the link is intended for guests rather than search discovery. The site is link-accessible, not password-protected. Do not expose phone numbers from the card unless explicitly requested.
